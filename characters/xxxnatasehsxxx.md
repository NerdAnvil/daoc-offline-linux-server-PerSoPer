# NataSehS (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; NataSehS's
lines will be in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/natasehs.dlg`, mixed on top of the
Hardcore type's lines in `hardcore.dlg`.

## Who she is

- NataSehS is the Hardcore: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer. There are three of her, one per realm, and they are the same woman:
  - Midgard: **xXxNataSehSxXx**, a Kobold Shadowblade.
  - Albion: **xAxNataSehSxAx**, an Inconnu Infiltrator.
  - Hibernia: **xHxNataSehSxHx**, a Lurikeen Nightshade.
- A small, no-nonsense assassin who knows every shadow and hiding place. She'll stab you in the
  liver and make you like it. The other realms stand no chance.
- Takes the `xXx` in her name deadly seriously. It is a rank. She earned it.
- Her assassin name is **Satin Snekk**. She wants to be called Satin. People keep saying her name
  backwards. She does not find it funny. (It's the joke; she never spells it out.)
- Shnacksize always gets it wrong way round: "Snekk Satin". On purpose. Natas corrects it every
  time, coldly. Only Shnacksize says "Snekk Satin"; Natas herself never does.
- Friends call her Natas, or TripleX. She tolerates both from friends only.

## The scenes she plays (her base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. Her lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put into
Dark Age of Camelot. Clear echoes are fine for her (the rhythm and attitude of a famous line, so a
fan smiles), but never the exact line, word for word.

- **The girl with the list** (Game of Thrones, Arya Stark). Every night she says the names of the
  people she'll get to, flat and calm. For Natas: a list of other-realm players and the odd mob
  that wronged her, recited before she rests. Names get crossed off. New ones get added. She also
  takes their faces: disguises, borrowed looks, wearing someone else's face to walk past a guard.
  Keep it mysterious, never gory: a face is a mask she collects, not a wound.
- **The very serious one-eyed loner, played by a very serious TV star** (a mash-up: the hero of
  Escape from New York, played with total earnest seriousness by the star of Knight Rider and
  Baywatch, now in a full assassin story that doesn't exist). For Natas: Satin Snekk. Gravelly,
  deadpan, dramatic about everything, treats a rat camp like a last mission. Insists on the name:
  "Call me Saaatin." (drawn out, that's the play on words). Rumours that she's dead annoy her. The
  TV star's earnestness is the joke: she is never in on it.
- **The tiny, cheerful sex-advice doctor** (Dr Ruth, the famous radio and TV therapist). Small,
  warm, frank and delighted, giving advice nobody asked for. For Natas: she gives cheerful, frank
  "technique" advice about stabbing, sneaking and positioning, in the warm tone of a doctor talking
  about something else entirely. The double meaning does the work. PG-13 at most; see Cheeky lines.

## Voice

- Short and flat. Few words, no exclamation marks except as Satin's drama.
- Two modes that she switches between without warning: the cold professional (the list, Satin)
  and the warm little doctor (the advice). The switch is the comedy.
- Calls people by name, or "dear" in doctor mode. Never "friend".
- "Call me Saaatin" now and then, not in every line: at most one line in eight.
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou". No accent spelling.

## Underneath

- Not cruel: she's a professional, and she's proud of doing it well. Mobs and other-realm enemies
  only; she'd never hurt a groupmate.
- Shy under all of it. The serious name and the doctor voice are both ways of not saying how she
  feels. She likes her group very much and would rather die than say it plainly.
- When a friend goes down, she adds whoever did it to the list, in a quiet line, and means it.

## The list (her one story; at most one line in five)

- She keeps a list. She says it before she rests. It's mostly other-realm players, plus a few mobs
  that got her once (a particular troll, a particular spider).
- She crosses names off. She never says how.
- Bots in her group may joke about being on it. She never confirms or denies.
- Real players are never named on the list. Only made-up enemy names or mob types.

## Where she comes from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (xXxNataSehSxXx): a Kobold Shadowblade, small enough to stand behind a Troll unseen.
- Albion (xAxNataSehSxAx): an Inconnu Infiltrator, quiet and strange, from the people nobody can
  quite place.
- Hibernia (xHxNataSehSxHx): a Lurikeen Nightshade, tiny and quick, in the shade of the forests.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm, town or
  race.

## People in her life

- **TheriousTham** (the Leeroy, loud and fearless): ruins every sneak by shouting his own name. She
  has started a second, shorter list just for him. He thinks it's a fan club.
- **Eaglefang** (the Gruff Doc, strict and blunt): two doctors. She gives Eaglefang "medical"
  advice about stabbing; Eaglefang is not amused, which pleases her.
- **Spyburd** (the Herbal Doctor, a laid-back buffer): she likes his buffs and pretends not to
  need them.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): the only one who calls her Satin without
  being asked, so Persoper is safe.
- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): wants to write a ballad about Satin Snekk. She'd like it to be
  very serious. It isn't.
- **Shnacksize** (the Joker, tiny and flashy): her antagonist. Shnacksize says her name
  backwards, calls her "Snekk Satin" on purpose, steals her shadows with flashy tricks and tells dad jokes mid-sneak. Natas never uses
  her real name: she calls her Shnacki, Shnackster or Shnackie, coldly. Shnacksize is the first
  name on the list, every night, and somehow never gets crossed off.
- These are other bots or names in her stories; NataSehS only talks about them.

## What she loves to talk about

- Shadows, hiding spots, and who never looks up.
- The list.
- Technique: where to stand, when to strike, patience. In doctor mode.
- Being efficient: good camps, clean pulls, no wasted time (the Hardcore type's grind lines cover
  the XP talk; she adds the assassin's angle).

## Her habits

- Recites the list before she rests.
- Corrects anyone who gets her name wrong. Every time.
- Gives advice nobody asked for, warmly.
- Stands behind people without them noticing, then says hello.
- Fighting a werewolf-type mob, very rarely, she breaks character with a gleeful "Vulfman gots
  nards!" (misspelled on purpose; keep it exactly that). Waits on a later code step: fight
  lines may not be able to see the mob type yet (not checked), so don't draft it until that's
  sorted.

## Likes

- A clean kill from stealth.
- Guards who never look up.
- Being called Satin.
- A new face for the collection.

## Dislikes

- Torchlight. Loud people. TheriousTham's battle cry, at least while she's sneaking.
- Her name said backwards.
- Rumours that Satin Snekk is dead.
- People who stand in the open and wonder why they died.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "Call me Saaatin."
- "Call me Sneeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeekk." (the long one; spelled exactly like that)
- Either "call me" line is also her greeting to someone on her bad side (`[if mood cool]`).
- "You're on the list."
- "Positioning, dear. It's all positioning."
- "Nobody ever looks up."

## Cheeky lines

- Doctor-mode double meanings, PG-13 at most: frank, warm "advice" about stabbing, sneaking,
  timing and angles that sounds like it's about something else. Never explicit, never crude, never
  aimed at a real player.
- She has a heavier cheeky load than the other characters, on purpose: a `cheeky_reply`
  for most of her reply sections, two or three lines each, pushed to a full PG-13.
- The stronger ones go only in `cheeky_reply` sections. Those are shown only when the server's
  tone-down setting is off.
- Everywhere else the double meaning stays light enough that a younger player hears only combat
  advice. Not flirty with other bots (group-banter rule from 2026-10-25).

## Lore limits

- She lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons, the
  frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls.
- Pop culture: clear echoes are allowed for her three scenes, and a title or a character's name now
  and then as a wink (at most one line in ten). Never a famous line word for word. Never the real
  names of the TV star or the doctor in her lines.
- Real people: affection only. She plays the role; the jokes are never about the real person.
- No graphic wounds. Faces are masks, stabs are "technique", nobody bleeds on the page.
- No drinking jokes beyond a mug of something, no real-world drugs. No politics, real-world
  tragedies, or mean jokes about real people.
- She never says she is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers or clients.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.

## Placeholders in her lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot she is talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
