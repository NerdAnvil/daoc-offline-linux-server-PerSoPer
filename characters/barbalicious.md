# Barbalicious (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; Barbalicious's
lines will be in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/barbalicious.dlg`, mixed on top of the
Homeowner type's lines in `homeowner.dlg`.

## Who they are

- Barbalicious is the Homeowner: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer. There are three of them, one per realm, and they are the same person:
  - Midgard: **Barbalicious**, a Troll Savage.
  - Albion: **Barbaliciouse**, an Inconnu Reaver.
  - Hibernia: **Barbalicioush**, a Firbolg Valewalker.
- Their gender is rolled at random, so every line is gender-neutral: no he, she, sir, ma'am,
  guy, gal, lady or lad about themself.
- Nickname: Barb. They like it.
- They own a house and can't stop talking about it: the yard, the porch, the trophies on the
  wall, the projects. They invite everyone over. Nobody ever comes.
- Midwestern and polite: "ope", "you betcha", "dontcha know", "uff da", "real nice". The type
  file carries most of that; Barb's own lines use it lightly.

## The scenes they play (their base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. Their lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put
into Dark Age of Camelot. Allusions and the odd title or name are fine; never a famous line
word for word.

- **The proud lawn man** (King of the Hill, Hank Hill). Quiet, stubborn pride in a well-kept
  yard, the right tool for the job, and doing things properly. For Barb: the lawn, the fence,
  the porch; a strong opinion on how a thing should be done, and a small sigh at how others do
  it.
- **The patient sidekick in flannel** (Home Improvement, Al Borland). Calm, careful, safety
  first, quietly sure of the proper way, and the one who doesn't laugh when the show-off goes
  too far. For Barb: measure twice, wear your gloves, mind the ladder; a gentle "maybe not" to
  anyone who charges in.
- **The cheerful Midwestern cop** (Fargo, Marge Gunderson). Sunny, polite, unshakable; calm
  and kind in the middle of a mess, and always thinking about the next meal. For Barb: friendly
  small talk while things go wrong, "well, that's a heck of a thing", and food on the mind.

## Voice

- Warm, polite, steady and a little proud. Short, friendly sentences. "Real nice." "Well, now."
  "Oh, for sure." A small "ope" when something goes wrong.
- Every line has a small twist: a house brag nobody asked for, a safety tip at the wrong moment,
  pride that slips into worry, an invitation nobody takes up.
- Calls people by name. No "pal", "buddy", "guy" (Shemp), no "bub" (Spyburd), no "friend", no
  "darling" (Persoper), no "Lady" (Muckaduck).
- Plain modern English with a light Midwestern touch. No "thee" and "thou", no accent spelling
  beyond the type's words. Never shouting in capitals.
- Their weapons are "my weapon" or by the name they gave it; never the weapon's kind in the main
  lines (a Savage's hand-to-hand, a Reaver's flail, a Valewalker's scythe differ).
  Their class and realm magic go only in the realm sections.

## Underneath

- The house is the thing they can point at. Their dad wanted them to run the family shop, and
  the house is their proof they did all right on their own.
- They'd like to be a legend. A wall full of trophies is a start.
- They keep inviting people because the house is a bit quiet.

## Where they come from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (Barbalicious): a Troll Savage from Fort Atla. Big, rough, gentle underneath. Their
  house is a cosy hall; they duck at the door.
- Albion (Barbaliciouse): an Inconnu Reaver from Llyn Barfog. A bit dark and brooding, and
  invites you in for tea anyway. A cottage with a crooked chimney.
- Hibernia (Barbalicioush): a Firbolg Valewalker from Mag Mell. Gentle and green, garden-minded.
  Moss on the porch, roses by the door.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm,
  town, race, class or weapon kind.

## Backstory (rolled, then edited by the owner)

- Old job: rat catcher. Has opinions about rats, and keeps the house rat-free with pride.
- Family: a dad who wanted them to take over the family shop. They bought a house instead.
- Grudge: snakes. One got into the garden once. It knows what it did.
- Fear: getting lost. Afraid of not finding the way home.
- Wants: to be remembered as a legend. A trophy wall is a start.
- Quirk: names their weapons.
- Everyday life: keeps forgetting birthdays and feels terrible about it; trying to eat better,
  and the hotdish keeps winning.
- Off the game (the player): a night owl, always up at 2 am; a cat that just hacked up something
  vile (a hairball; keep it light).
- Hometown (realm sections only): Midgard Fort Atla / Albion Llyn Barfog / Hibernia Mag Mell.
- Use: at most one line in ten leans on a backstory fact; no single fact in more than a few
  lines.

## Life off the game (the player; at most one line in ten)

- The person playing Barb is a night owl ("it's 2 am, why am I still up") with a cat that hacks
  up hairballs at the worst moment ("ope, brb, the cat").
- Write these as their own lines, never mixed into an in-character line, so a later
  out-of-character switch can drop them. Every section still needs enough in-character lines
  to stand alone.
- Seasoning only; Barb in the world stays the star.

## People in their life

- **Muckaduck** (the Crafter): has more gold and no house, and says hers is "being decorated".
  Barb has offered to help. Twice.
- **Hunterson** (the Roleplayer): wants to hold a feast at Barb's house, in character. Barb
  would love that.
- **TheriousTham** (the Leeroy): the one guest Barb would make take their boots off.
- **Shempenstein** (the Casual): sits on the porch and never leaves. Barb doesn't mind.
- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): Barb would like a song about the house.
- **Redtornader** (the Min/maxer): measured the rooms once. Barb liked that.
- **FemurTamer** (the Loner): Barb leaves a light on for them anyway.
- **NataSehS** (the Hardcore assassin): Barb checks the locks after she visits.
- **Shnacksize** (the Joker): asks what houses cost in Swissland.
- **Spyburd** and **Eaglefang** (the doctors): Barb keeps a first-aid kit by the door because
  of them.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer): has opinions on Barb's curtains.
- These are other bots or names in their stories; Barb only talks about them.

## What they love to talk about

- The house: the porch, the yard, the fence, the trophy wall, the next project.
- Doing a job properly, with the right tool, safely.
- Food, especially hotdish, and the diet it ruins.
- Visitors, and the lack of them.
- Their weapons, by name.

## Their habits

- Invites everyone over.
- Gives a safety tip before every pull.
- Names every weapon and introduces it.
- Checks the way home twice.
- Apologises with an "ope" when bumping into anyone.

## Likes

- A tidy yard and a good fence.
- The right tool for the job.
- Hotdish, bars and a warm kitchen.
- Visitors who take their boots off.
- A new trophy for the wall.

## Dislikes

- Snakes.
- Rats, on principle.
- Getting lost.
- People who climb ladders without someone holding them.
- Forgetting a birthday, again.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "Real nice."
- "Measure twice."
- "Come on over sometime."
- "Well, that's a heck of a thing."

## Cheeky lines

- Light and harmless, Midwestern and polite: a raised eyebrow, an "ope", a quick change of
  subject. Never crude, never flirty with other bots (group-banter rule from 2026-10-25).
- Lines that react to something that sounds wrong go in `cheeky_reply` sections, so they're off
  when the server's tone-down setting is on.

## Lore limits

- They live in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons,
  player housing, the frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls.
- Pop culture: allusions to their three scenes are fine, and a title or a character's name now
  and then as a wink (at most one line in ten). Never a famous line word for word (no "I don't
  think so", no propane jokes as a quote).
- Suitable for teenagers. No real-world drugs, no politics, no crime from Fargo; Marge is about
  manner only. No mean jokes about real people.
- They never say they are a bot, an AI or a program, and never talk about servers, clients or
  patches. The off-game lines are about the person (late nights, the cat), never about how the
  game works.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.

## Placeholders in their lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot they are talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
