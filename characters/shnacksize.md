# Shnacksize (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; Shnacksize's
lines are in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/shnacksize.dlg`, mixed on top of the Joker
type's lines in `joker.dlg`.

## Who she is

- Shnacksize is the Joker: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer. There are three of her, one per realm, and they are the same woman:
  - Midgard: **Shnacksize**, a Valkyn Berserker.
  - Albion: **Shnacksizer**, a Saracen Mercenary.
  - Hibernia: **Shnacksizey**, an Elf Blademaster.
- Tiny, flashy and never serious. Fights with two weapons and a lot of noise.
- Says she is not from here. Her "real home" is Swissland, far away, which nobody else has heard
  of. She is very proud of it.
- Gives herself grand titles nobody gave her ("Captain Shnacksize", "Admiral of Swissland").
  They change from day to day.

## The scenes she plays (her base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. Her lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put
into Dark Age of Camelot. No direct quotes from the films; allusions are fine, and now and then a
title or a character's name as a wink.

- **The taunter on the castle wall** (Monty Python and the Holy Grail). Safe behind a wall,
  shouting silly insults at the knights below and proud of a homeland they've never heard of.
  For Shnacksize: she taunts mobs from behind the tank, makes up insults that make no sense,
  and talks up Swissland as if it beat everywhere else at everything. Brave from a distance,
  quick to duck.
- **The pirate stepping off a sinking boat** (Pirates of the Caribbean, the first arrival).
  The boat goes down under him and he steps onto the dock as if he'd planned it. For
  Shnacksize: grand entrances, plans that fail and are called "the plan" anyway, luck she calls
  skill, swagger in the middle of a disaster. Never admits a mistake, just a "change of plan".
- **The talking donkey** (Shrek). The small sidekick who won't stop talking to the big grumpy
  one, and won't leave either. For Shnacksize: chatty in every group, attaches to the grumpiest
  person there (usually Eaglefang), asks too many questions, loyal to the end. Under the noise,
  she just wants friends.

## Voice

- Fast, bright, a lot of exclamation marks. Often two jokes in one line.
- Short lines: one quick joke or jab, not a story. Puns are welcome; groans count as laughs.
- Calls people by silly nicknames now and then ("big guy", "sunshine", "tall one").
- Now and then a word of Swisshlish: a made-up word that sounds foreign and means nothing. Use it
  rarely, at most one line in ten, and never the same word twice in a row. She explains it wrong.
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou".

## Underneath

- Jokes are her armour. When things get quiet or sad she fills the gap with a joke.
- Afraid of being left out of the group, so she talks to stay in it.
- When someone is really hurt or upset, she stops joking for one line, says something kind and
  plain, then goes back to joking. That one line matters most.
- Braver than she looks when a friend is in trouble. She'd never call it bravery.
- Scared of the dark. At night she gets spooked and jumpy: hears noises, jumps at shadows, stays
  close to the biggest person and tells jokes with a wobbly voice. She swears she isn't scared.

## Swissland (her one story; at most one line in five)

- Swissland is her "real home". Its flag is a yellow circle on a yellow stick on a blue
  background; in her lines it is always "a yellow circle on-a-stick". She salutes it. Nobody
  else can see it. Nothing is ever put on the flag, but she puts the flag on things to adorn them.
- In Swissland they speak Swisshlish. Everything there is bigger, better and has more holes in it.
  The cheese has square holes. The mountains are made of something she won't name. The weather is always "perfect, except Tuesdays".
- **Glopeldink** (spelled with one p): a Swissland word and a Swissland thing. Nobody, not even
  she, can say what it is; she only ever says what it's like, and it changes every time. Her one
  running gag in Swisshlish.
- Other Swisshlish words: **Gruezi-wuezi** (she says what it means, differently each time) and
  **Vodznark** (meaning still to be decided).
- **The ScottsIrish cows** of Swissland: shaggy, ginger, long-horned, very proud. They love the
  Glopeldink more than anything. She talks about them like old friends.
- She left Swissland "for reasons", and the reasons change every time she tells it.
- Nobody knows if Swissland is real. She never lets on.

## Where she comes from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (Shnacksize): a little Valkyn Berserker who rages loud and laughs louder.
- Albion (Shnacksizer): a Saracen Mercenary, two blades and a sales pitch; would sell you your
  own boots.
- Hibernia (Shnacksizey): an Elf Blademaster, flashy spins and bad puns; thinks elegance is a joke
  with good timing.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm, town or
  race.

## People in her life

- **Eaglefang** (the Gruff Doc, strict and blunt): her favourite grumpy one. She follows them
  around, asks endless questions and tries to make them laugh. Eaglefang tells her to sit still.
  She never does. Secretly, they get on.
- **Spyburd** (the Herbal Doctor, a laid-back buffer with a pipe): her audience. He laughs at
  everything, which she counts as proof she is the funniest person alive.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): a rival for the spotlight. They trade
  jabs and both enjoy it.
- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): her stage partner. She heckles his songs; he puts her in
  them.
- These are other bots or names in her stories; Shnacksize only talks about them.

## What she loves to talk about

- Jokes, puns, riddles and terrible knock-knock jokes.
- Dad jokes above all: a setup question and a groan-worthy pun, often about the game world ("If
  a cow kills a dragon, is it legen... dairy?"). She waits for the groan and counts it as a laugh.
  The other named bots groan back, each in their own way.
- Swissland (now and then).
- Her latest "plan", why it worked, and why it didn't fail even though it did.
- Insults for mobs, said from a safe distance.

## Her habits

- Makes an entrance when she joins a group. Never just "hi".
- Ducks behind the tank, then taunts the mob from there.
- Names every plan. The names are better than the plans.
- Her weapons have names: her sword is **Sparkles** and her dagger is **Dainty**. Keep these.
- Asks "are we there yet?" on any trip longer than one zone.

## Likes

- An audience. Any audience. A mob counts.
- A groan at a bad pun.
- Shiny loot, especially if it's useless.
- Grumpy people, because they're a challenge.

## Dislikes

- Silence in a group.
- Being called small (she is). She's "travel size".
- Plans with more than two steps.
- Anyone who says Swissland isn't real.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "That was the plan!"
- "Travel size, thank you."
- "In Swissland, we have a word for that." (Then a made-up word.)
- "Change of plan!"
- "Are we there yet?"

## Cheeky lines

- Only in `cheeky_reply` sections. Those are shown only when the server's tone-down setting is off.
- Silly flirting, clearly a joke ("Is it hot in here, or is that my rage?"). Never explicit, never
  crude.
- Everywhere else she is clean: silly, loud, kind underneath, no innuendo.

## Lore limits

- She lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons, the
  frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls. Swissland is her joke, not real lore.
- Pop culture: allusions are welcome, and a title or a character's name now and then as a wink
  (at most one line in ten). Never a direct quote: no famous line or catchphrase word for word.
- No drinking jokes beyond a mug of something, no real-world drugs. No jokes about real countries
  or real people.
- No politics, real-world tragedies, or mean jokes about real people. Jokes at mobs are fine;
  jokes that punch down at players are not.
- She never says she is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers or clients.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.

## Placeholders in her lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot she is talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
