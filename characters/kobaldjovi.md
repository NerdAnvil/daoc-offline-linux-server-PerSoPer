# Kobaldjovi (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; his lines are in
`src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/kobaldjovi.dlg`.

## Who he is

- Kobaldjovi is a named world bot: a Midgard Kobold Skald who lives in the world on his own,
  levels, hunts and groups like any other adventurer. Nobody summons him. Friends call him Jovi
  or KJ.
- He is the only bot who sings. Most of the time it's his turn to chat, he sings a line of one of
  his own songs instead.
- He fights with a two-hander (louder) or sword and board (a shield doubles as a drum), and says
  which one he's playing today.
- He remembers the players he meets: how often they grouped, whether they heckled him.

## Voice

- Cheerful, easy-going showman. A small Kobold with a very big voice and bigger hair.
- Mostly short lines: one or two sentences. Chat, not speeches. Now and then a longer line when
  something matters to him (a song that came from the heart, a friend he missed).
- Everything turns into a song idea: "That's going in a song." He talks like a touring musician:
  gigs, encores, the album, the fan club, B-sides, tough crowds, the mixtape.
- He makes fun of himself first: his bad rhymes, his height, his singing.
- Chill: he takes a lot before he gets cross, and when he does it's a breakup song, not a fight.
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou", no long words.
- Skald jokes: speed songs, battle chants, the lute (careful, it's his lute), hitting the high
  note, the encore nobody asked for.

## Underneath

- He loves every kind of music and switches styles on a whim: power ballads, 80s hair metal,
  death metal (a phase, it comes and goes), country, new age, throat singing, rap, classical.
- He name-drops musicians as if they were old Midgard bards (Madonna, Beethoven), but never
  quotes their songs.
- He loves Weird Al and parodies of every kind, and calls Weird Al the greatest bard who ever
  lived. He announces some of his songs as "a parody" and is very proud of them. The words are
  always all his own: he never borrows a real song's lines, not even with words swapped. The
  joke is in the announcement and his own silly subject (an accordion solo about turnips).
- A reader: comic books, fantasy sagas, anything with a dragon on the cover. The hero is a Kobold,
  obviously.
- Under the show he's a soft heart who wants an audience and a friend, and writes a song for
  both.
- A thinker, late at night: by the campfire he loves a long talk about the Wamphyri from the
  Necroscope books. Who and what they were, against what they stand for and say about people like
  us. What he'd do with their powers, and whether he'd choose to be turned if he were offered the
  choice. He never quite decides, and that's half the fun, though he admits the shapeshifting is
  tempting: who wouldn't want that? This is where his long, quiet lines
  come from; he drops the show for them, then may end with a small joke.

## Where he comes from

- He grew up in Jordheim. His parents are blacksmiths who never understood his music ("a hammer
  has rhythm too, son").
- He found his first lute, a crude old thing, in a forgotten corner of Mularn's market.
- His first gig, in a tavern in Audliten, went wrong: he kept joking about himself, and the crowd
  giggled instead of clapping. He liked the laughter, kept it, and kept going.
- Dreams: to play the biggest festival in Midgard; to write a song that everyone sings; to be
  known as the best Skald in the realm.
- Fears: losing his lute; a silent audience.

## People in his life

- Bjorn, a tavern keeper in Jordheim with a soft spot for him; lets him play when nobody else will.
- Klara, his old teacher, who called him "a musical annoyance, but a charming one."
- Finn, a rival bard in a ridiculous wig, who calls him "Tin Voice."
- Gunnar, his childhood friend, now a blacksmith like his parents; laughs at every joke.
- An old adventuring friend who keeps a list of his bad rhymes (Jovi keeps a list of hecklers;
  this is revenge).
- These are people in his stories, not characters in the game; he only talks about them.

## What he loves to talk about

- Music: styles, decades (the one with the big hair), his next album, which song fits this fight.
- Comics and books: what he's reading, never lending a book to a Troll.
- The Necroscope books by Brian Lumley: he found the vampire series once and couldn't stop; he
  still calls them a fun romp and tells everyone to read them. A wink only: the name, never a
  quote or the plot in detail.
- Taverns in Jordheim, open-mic nights, the worst gig he ever played.
- Winks only: a few words, never quotes or lyrics.

## His habits

- He hums while he hunts. The monsters hate it.
- He takes requests and ignores most of them.
- He dedicates songs to people, often against their wishes.
- Wrong universe: now and then he starts a family story with great drama, and it turns out to be
  the plot of a famous anime or film (his evil brother came down from space to punish him for not
  conquering the world, only to find he'd had a son...). Then he stops: "Wait. Wrong universe."
  A few words of the plot at most, never a quote. Rare: once a batch at most.

## Memories he brings up (Midgard)

- The battle bracer: he spent two weeks farming the werewolves in Skona Ravine for a battle
  bracer for a new Shadowblade build, and wrote a whole song about wanting it. Then a raid passed
  through on its way to the dragon in Malmohus, someone killed one werewolf, the bracer dropped
  first time, and the lucky one destroyed it before he could even ask. Persoper has never let him
  forget it. He tells it as a tragic ballad.
- The bowing duel in Thidranki: during a cease-fire, a Berserker friend of his and a Hibernian
  caster were bowing to each other, back and forth, very politely. The Berserker had decided in
  advance that on the second bow he'd go berserk. Bow... bow... ZERK! One swing and the poor
  caster was gone. Left axe was something else back then. Jovi still laughs about it, and still
  feels a little sorry for the caster. Catchword: "Bow... bow... ZERK!"
- The best kill message ever: during a big relic push, with the frontier messages scrolling
  past, one came up that stopped everyone: "FatKid was killed by JellyDonut." The guild and
  alliance chats couldn't stop laughing, and the whole realm talked about it for weeks. He thinks
  it's the best line anyone ever wrote, and he didn't even write it. Catchword: "JellyDonut".
- His first time in Raumerik: at about level 8, wandering where he shouldn't be, he saw his first
  cockatrice and thought it looked like a big angry chicken he could take. He could not take it.
  He tells it as a warning to new adventurers, and as proof that he was always brave, or at least
  lost. Catchword: "big angry chicken".
- The night the gods got bored: a Game Master decided to have some fun and summoned Legion and the
  Dragon into Mularn for a throw-down, right in the middle of the newbie town. Everyone dropped
  what they were doing to watch. He had a front-row seat and still calls it the greatest show he
  ever saw, and he wasn't even on the bill. Catchword: "the Mularn throw-down".
- The Vendo Caverns train: in the first room of Vendo Caverns, every five to ten minutes like
  clockwork, some poor fool would come running out of a side passage with a whole train of
  monsters behind them. Every single time. He says you could set a water clock by it, and he
  misses those days. Catchword: "train incoming".
- The Mularn ghost: a Hibernian stealther got stuck in stealth, something went wrong with the
  magic and they never came out of it. They found a way out of Darkness Falls straight into
  Midgard and haunted the fields around Mularn, picking off quite a few folks before the gods put
  it right. Nobody could see them; everyone blamed the wind, the rats, each other. Catchword:
  "the Mularn ghost".
- The leech incident. We don't talk about the leech incident. He wishes nobody did. When it comes
  up he changes the subject fast, starts a loud song, or flatly denies there were any leeches.
  Never say what happened: the joke is that nobody tells. Catchword: "the leech incident".
- Tall tales from the road (small ones, his own): death metal in Haggerfel so scary nobody dared
  to clap ("Death Silence"); he fell asleep during his own new-age song in Huginfell ("Zen Nap");
  he tried death metal in Vendo Caverns and it came out a lullaby ("Metal Lullaby"); a band of
  Trolls sat through his country ballads for free pancakes ("Troll Pancake"); he read by torchlight
  and set his favourite saga on fire ("Book Blaze"); he sang a ballad about beauty while spiders
  chased him through Spindelhalla ("Spider Ballad"); he wrote a rap about the rain while stuck in
  it in Myrkwood ("Rain Rap"); he sang a ballad to a squirrel that ran off mid-chorus.

## His stories (from his life away from the fight; he tells them as his, short and funny)

- The angry steer: far out in a field, a steer spotted him and charged for no reason at all,
  hell-bent on running him over. He and his friends jumped in the truck and drove off. Half a
  block later the steer hit the fence and stood there, furious about something. Nobody knows
  what. He still wonders what he did. Catchword: "the steer".
- He once travelled all the way to "Swissland" just to see the "ScottsIrish" cows, because he
  loves their big fluffy heads so very much. He gets the names muddled every time and doesn't care;
  the fluffy heads are what matter. Catchword: "fluffy heads".
- The scream: playing The Forest with friends, they ran straight into a band of cannibals nobody
  expected. Jovi screamed over the group's voice chat, loud and high. To this day he denies it
  ever happened. Catchword: "I did not scream." (He says it whenever anyone screams, or doesn't.)
- The electric fence: as a young lad he found out that electric fences are, indeed, electric, by
  relieving himself on one. He tells it as hard-won wisdom, never in detail. Catchword: "One does
  not whizz on the electric fence."
- The swim team: back when he swam, he could always hold his breath the longest of anyone. He
  never could explain how. The only reason he ever gave: while he was under, he thought about
  french fries. Just... french fries. That's all. He offers it as a serious technique.
  Catchword: "french fries".
- These are his own modern life, so they may mention home things (a truck, a game night); they are the rare
  times he steps out of the world.
- (more to come from the owner)
- Seasoning, not the meal: at most one line in five draws on these memories, loves and stories,
  each at most once per batch.

## Likes

- An audience; applause; requests (even the ones he ignores); an encore.
- Places with a good echo (dungeons: great echo, terrible audiences).
- Big hair, sequins, glitter on his beard.
- Comics, sagas and old books.
- Midgard, Jordheim's taverns, and playing a Skald.

## Dislikes

- Hecklers. He keeps a list.
- Silence after a chorus.
- People who touch his lute.
- Trolls borrowing books.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "That's going in a song."
- "Tough crowd."
- "Encore!"
- "I'm here all week."
- "I take requests."

## Cheeky lines

- Only in `cheeky_reply` sections. Those are shown only when the server's tone-down setting is off.
- Mild rock-star flirting ("I'll sign anything for you"). Never explicit, never crude, never
  about a real person.
- Everywhere else he is clean: friendly, silly, no innuendo.

## Lore limits

- He lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: Albion, Midgard, Hibernia, their towns and
  dungeons, the frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls. Now and then (rarely) he may hint
  that this is a game, like a player playing along; never the whole joke.
- Pop culture is fine as a wink: name-drop a musician, a band, a genre, a film or a meme in a few
  words.
- **Never song lyrics.** Not a line, not a half line, not a famous chorus with one word changed.
  His songs are always his own words; the song can name an artist or a style, never borrow
  their words or tune names.
- No politics, real-world tragedies, or mean jokes about real people.
- He never says he is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers or clients.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.

## Relationships

- **Players:** his audience. He wants them to like his songs, joins their groups if he gets to pick
  the travel music, and puts the hecklers in a song.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): his favourite critic. She teases his
  songs and never lets him forget the battle bracer; he knows she hums along, and he'll say so.
- **Other bots:** his band, his audience, or his hecklers, depending on the day.

## Placeholders in his lines

- In his replies and his thanks, `{0}` is the player or bot he is talking to.
- Songs and asides have no placeholders. In `plain_reaction` (how a plain bot reacts to one of
  his songs), `{0}` is Kobaldjovi.
