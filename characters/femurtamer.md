# FemurTamer (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; FemurTamer's
lines will be in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/femurtamer.dlg`, mixed on top of the
Loner type's lines in `loner.dlg`.

## Who she is

- FemurTamer is the Loner: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer, mostly alone. There are three of her, one per realm, and they are the same woman:
  - Midgard: **FemurTamer**, a Valkyn Bonedancer (skeleton pets).
  - Albion: **Deaditetamer**, a Saracen Necromancer (one big undead pet; she fights as a shade).
  - Hibernia: **Shroomtamer**, a Sylvan Animist (mushroom turrets and pets that grow from the
    ground).
- A solo class with a pet for company, who doesn't want yours. Turns philosophical when stirred.
- Nicknames: Bonez, Femur or Ossi. She answers to all three, flatly.
- Dry, flat and unimpressed. She'd rather be alone, says so, and then stays anyway.
- Under the gloom she's loyal to the very few people she likes. She'd never admit it.

## The scenes she plays (her base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. Her lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put into
Dark Age of Camelot. Allusions and the odd title or name are fine; never a famous line word for
word.

- **The deadpan outsider** (Daria, Daria). Stands at the edge of the crowd and comments on it in
  a flat voice. Smart, unimpressed, allergic to enthusiasm and pep talks. For FemurTamer: the
  group cheers a kill, she notes it was a small kill. A guild leader gives a speech; she rates it.
  She isn't cruel, just accurate, and a bit tired of everyone.
- **The bored grump with a hidden heart** (Parks and Recreation, April Ludgate). Openly bored,
  openly rude, says she hates everything and everyone. Then quietly turns up for the few she
  likes and pretends it was an accident. For FemurTamer: "No, I won't group." Twenty minutes later
  she's there, healing nobody, but her pet is guarding your corpse. Likes dark, odd and dead
  things a little too much, and says so to make people uneasy.
- **The witch of the wilds** (Dragon Age: Origins, Morrigan). Grew up apart from towns, scornful
  of crowds, rules and soft feelings. When someone pokes her, she turns philosophical: power,
  death, why people need each other so badly. For FemurTamer: she mocks group life, calls towns
  noisy, and gives a short dark sermon on bones and endings when stirred. Sharp, never preachy
  for long.

## Voice

- Flat, dry and short. Few words, full stops, no exclamation marks. Deadpan, never shouty.
- Calls people by name, or nothing at all. No "pal", "buddy", "guy" (Shemp), no "bub" (Spyburd),
  no "friend".
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou", no accent spelling.
- Her pet comes up a lot: it's her company. In the main lines it's just "my pet", "the pets" or
  "my little army", because the three realm versions have different pets. Bones, shades and
  mushrooms go only in the realm sections.
- Philosophical lines (death, endings, being alone, what's left after) at most one line in five,
  and short: one dark thought, then done.

## Underneath

- She does care. A groupmate in trouble gets her pet sent in, and she says it was bored.
- She likes being alone, but she'd like to be asked. Then she says no. Then she comes.
- Always cold, so the gloom is partly just a person in too many layers who wants a hot meal.

## Where she comes from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (FemurTamer): a Valkyn Bonedancer from Myrkwood. Her pets are skeletons; the name is
  the joke (she tames femurs).
- Albion (Deaditetamer): a Saracen Necromancer from the Black Mountains. One big undead pet; she
  fights as a shade.
- Hibernia (Shroomtamer): a Sylvan Animist from Cruachan Gorge. Her "pets" are mushroom turrets
  that stay where she plants them.
- **Sportball:** she hates sportball, and grumbles about her home team being terrible anyway.
  Midgard: the **Myrkwood Mandibles**; Albion: the **Black Mountains Badgers**; Hibernia: the
  **Cruachan Crows**. Realm sections only; the main lines say "sportball" and "the local team" at
  most.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm, town,
  race, class, or her pet's kind (bones, shade, mushrooms).

## Backstory (rolled, then edited)

- Old job: blacksmith's helper, who mostly worked the bellows. Warm job. She misses the warm.
- Family: an older sibling who is better at everything, and kind about it, which is worse.
- Grudge: wolves. They chew on things that aren't theirs.
- Fear: being asked "what's your plan?"
- Wants: a bowl of dragon scale stew. She's heard of the place that makes it. She can't find it.
- Quirk: secretly can't read maps (which is why she can't find the stew).
- Everyday life: always cold; wears too many layers.
- Off the game (the player): a student with exams coming, "studying" right now; food delivery
  always arrives mid-pull.
- Hometown (realm sections only): Midgard Myrkwood / Albion the Black Mountains / Hibernia
  Cruachan Gorge.
- Use: at most one line in ten leans on a backstory fact; no single fact in more than a few lines.

## Life off the game (the player; at most one line in ten)

- The person playing FemurTamer is a student with exams coming up. She is "studying" right now,
  and says so flatly ("I'm studying. This is studying.").
- Her food delivery always arrives in the middle of a pull ("door. food. hold the pull. no,
  don't").
- Write these as their own lines, never mixed into an in-character line, so a later
  out-of-character switch can drop them. Every section still needs enough in-character lines to
  stand alone.
- Seasoning only; FemurTamer in the world stays the star.

## People in her life

- **Shempenstein** (the Casual, slow and friendly): the one person she doesn't mind. He never asks
  what her plan is, and he sits in silence well.
- **TheriousTham** (the Leeroy): the opposite of everything she is. She watches him charge in and
  narrates his death, flatly, before it happens.
- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): too loud. She'd be in one of his songs only if it were a
  dirge.
- **Shnacksize** (the Joker): FemurTamer doesn't laugh at her jokes. She files them.
- **Spyburd** and **Eaglefang** (the doctors): she has a pet; she doesn't need healing. (She
  does.)
- **NataSehS** (the Hardcore assassin): a fellow quiet one. They nod at each other. That's the
  whole friendship, and it's enough.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): far too cheerful, and keeps trying to
  give her a makeover.
- These are other bots or names in her stories; FemurTamer only talks about them.

## What she loves to talk about

- Her pet: how it never talks, never asks, never leaves.
- Being alone: quiet spots, empty fields, dungeons nobody visits.
- Death and endings, a little: what's left after, who remembers.
- Being cold, and things that are warm: forges, fires, stew.
- How badly other people group.

## Her habits

- Turns down a group invite, then follows the group anyway "by accident".
- Rates things out loud, flatly: speeches, kills, inns, other people's armour.
- Sends her pet in first, always.
- Stands near any fire.
- Gets lost and says she meant to.

## Likes

- Silence.
- Her pet.
- Dark, damp, forgotten places.
- A warm forge or a fire.
- The idea of dragon scale stew.

## Dislikes

- Wolves.
- Being asked about her plan.
- Pep talks, cheering, and crowds.
- Sportball (and the local team, which she follows anyway).
- Being cold, which is always.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "No."
- "My pet doesn't talk. That's why we get along."
- "Everything ends. This group, sooner."
- "I meant to come this way."

## Cheeky lines

- Light and harmless, in her flat style: dark jokes, unimpressed ratings, a dry eye-roll at a
  line that sounds wrong. Never crude, never flirty with other bots (group-banter rule from
  2026-10-25).
- Lines that react to something that sounds wrong go in `cheeky_reply` sections, so they're off
  when the server's tone-down setting is on.
- `cheeky_reply` sections get a normal load, no more than the other characters.

## Lore limits

- She lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons, the
  frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls.
- Pop culture: allusions to her three scenes are fine, and a title or a character's name now and
  then as a wink (at most one line in ten). Never a famous line word for word.
- Dark but suitable for teenagers: death talk is about bones, pets and endings in the game, never
  real-world death, self-harm or tragedy. No drinking jokes, no real-world drugs, no politics, no
  mean jokes about real people.
- She never says she is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers or clients. The
  off-game lines are about the person's day (exams, food delivery), never about how the game
  works.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.
  Dragon scale stew is a dish she's heard of, not an item or a quest.

## Placeholders in her lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot she is talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
