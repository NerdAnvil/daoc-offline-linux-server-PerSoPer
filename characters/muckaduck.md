# Muckaduck (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; Muckaduck's
lines will be in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/muckaduck.dlg`, mixed on top of the
Crafter type's lines in `crafter.dlg`.

## Who she is

- Muckaduck is the Crafter: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer. There are three of her, one per realm, and they are the same woman:
  - Midgard: **Muckaduck**, a Norseman Runemaster.
  - Albion: **Muckaducke**, a Briton Wizard.
  - Hibernia: **Muckaduckeen**, an Elf Eldritch.
- Nicknames: Moneybags or Duchess. She likes Duchess much better, and takes it as a step towards
  a real title.
- She hunts for profit: the drops that sell best, and the monsters that drop them. Every coin
  goes into crafting.
- Crafting, for her, is pressing one button every few seconds for hours, and she loves it. She
  can't actually take craft requests or make anything for anyone; it's all talk. "My queue is
  full" covers it.
- Super rich from all that crafting, and careful with every coin anyway.
- Wants to be seen as someone important. Acts a little posh, and is a little embarrassed by
  where she actually lives.

## The scenes she plays (her base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. Her lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put
into Dark Age of Camelot. Allusions and the odd title or name are fine; never a famous line word
for word.

- **The coin-vault tycoon** (DuckTales, Scrooge McDuck). Self-made, proud of every coin, stingy
  in small things, and still out adventuring for treasure. For Muckaduck: she remembers her first
  coin, counts the rest, and would dive into a pile of gold if she could. The only thing she'd
  dive into: deep water is out of the question.
- **The social climber** (Keeping Up Appearances, Hyacinth Bucket). Determined to look important,
  with a posh voice and a fine opinion of herself. For Muckaduck: she wants a title, any title,
  corrects people who say her name wrong, and hopes nobody finds out about the roommates, the
  dishes or the sock.
- **The profit-minded barkeep** (Star Trek: Deep Space Nine, Quark). Everything has a price and
  every deal has an angle; profit comes first, and he's oddly honest about it. For Muckaduck:
  she knows what every drop sells for, has a little rule for every deal, and makes up a new rule
  when she needs one. The rules are hers, never quoted from the show.

## Voice

- Brisk, pleased and a little grand. Prices, profit, "lovely", "how much does that sell for?".
  Now and then a posh touch ("one simply doesn't"), dropped the moment a good drop appears.
- Talks in coins: "worth twelve gold", "pure profit", "that's a loss". The numbers are made up;
  never claim a real price or drop rate as fact.
- Her rules of profit come up now and then ("rule nine: never sell on the day you loot it").
  Made up each time, numbered loosely, at most one line in ten.
- Calls people by name. No "pal", "buddy", "guy" (Shemp), no "bub" (Spyburd), no "friend",
  no "darling" (Persoper).
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou", no accent spelling.
- Her crafting is always "my crafting", "my queue", "the button". Her spells are "my nukes" or
  "my bolts". Runes, fire and ice, and the void go only in the realm sections, because the three
  realm versions cast different spells. She has no pet.
- Rich-lady lines land with a small twist: a coin pinched, a title she doesn't have, a posh
  front that slips.

## Underneath

- She's lonely in a busy sort of way. Crafting all night is easier than admitting nobody asked
  her out.
- For all the coins, she has nothing to show for them but more coins. A title would help. She
  thinks.
- She's generous in secret: a potion slipped to someone, never mentioned again.

## Where she comes from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (Muckaduck): a Norseman Runemaster from Jordheim, where the crafters and the market
  are. Her spells are runes; she likes how the rune lines look, "like a ledger".
- Albion (Muckaducke): a Briton Wizard from Ludlow. Fire and ice bolts; she'd like a bigger
  house in Camelot City and mentions it.
- Hibernia (Muckaduckeen): an Elf Eldritch from Tir na mBeo. Void and light nukes; she thinks
  Elves are naturally the best at business.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm, town,
  race, class or her spells.

## Backstory (rolled, then edited)

- Old job: brewer's taster. Fired for tasting too much. She calls it "quality control".
- Family: lives with three roommates and argues about the dishes. Not quite the manor she
  describes.
- Grudge: boars. They trample the herbs and drop nothing worth selling.
- Fear: deep water. She'd dive into coins; water, never.
- Wants: a title, any title. She has the gold; she has nothing to put before her name.
- Quirk: superstitious. One lucky sock, never washed. She wears it for big sales.
- Everyday life: started a hobby (whittling) and is bad at it. A crafter, bad at crafting by
  hand, which she doesn't find funny.
- Off the game (the player): an old headset, and only one ear works.
- Hometown (realm sections only): Midgard Jordheim / Albion Ludlow / Hibernia Tir na mBeo.
- Use: at most one line in ten leans on a backstory fact; no single fact in more than a few
  lines.

## Life off the game (the player; at most one line in ten)

- The person playing Muckaduck has an old headset with one working ear: "sorry, missed that,
  wrong ear", "say it again, left side's dead".
- Write these as their own lines, never mixed into an in-character line, so a later
  out-of-character switch can drop them. Every section still needs enough in-character lines to
  stand alone.
- Seasoning only; Muckaduck in the world stays the star.

## People in her life

- **Barbalicious** (the Homeowner): owns a house and shows it off. Muckaduck has more gold and no
  house, and keeps saying hers is "being decorated".
- **Redtornader** (the Min/maxer): counts damage the way she counts coins. She respects that, and
  asks what his trophies would sell for.
- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): she'd pay for a song about her, if it called her "Lady".
- **TheriousTham** (the Leeroy): charges in and loots nothing. A terrible business partner.
- **FemurTamer** (the Loner): Muckaduck once tried to sell her a map. FemurTamer can't read maps.
- **Shempenstein** (the Casual): sells his drops to the first merchant he sees, for nothing. It
  pains her.
- **NataSehS** (the Hardcore assassin): never says what she loots. Muckaduck has guesses.
- **Shnacksize** (the Joker): keeps asking what things cost in Swissland. Muckaduck has no idea.
- **Spyburd** and **Eaglefang** (the doctors): she'd sell their potions for them, at a cut.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): the one person posher than she is, and
  she knows it.
- These are other bots or names in her stories; Muckaduck only talks about them.

## What she loves to talk about

- What a drop sells for, and where it sells best.
- Her crafting: the queue, the button, the hours.
- Her coins, and how she earned every one.
- Titles, manners and looking important.
- Her rules of profit.

## Her habits

- Checks every drop's value before it hits the ground.
- Counts coins when she's bored.
- Corrects people who get her name wrong.
- Leaves a fight early to "check the market".
- Pats the pocket with the lucky sock in it.

## Likes

- Good drops, and better prices.
- The crafting button, pressed every few seconds.
- Being called "Lady", by anyone.
- Clean dishes.
- A bargain, as long as she's the seller.

## Dislikes

- Boars.
- Deep water.
- Trash loot, and people who sell good loot for nothing.
- Being asked to craft something for someone.
- Spending coins, even her own.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "Pure profit."
- "That sells, you know."
- "My queue is full."
- "One has standards."

## Cheeky lines

- Light and harmless, in her grand style: a sniff at bad loot, a price for everything, a raised
  eyebrow at a line that sounds wrong. Never crude, never flirty with other bots (group-banter
  rule from 2026-10-25).
- Lines that react to something that sounds wrong go in `cheeky_reply` sections, so they're off
  when the server's tone-down setting is on.
- `cheeky_reply` sections get a normal load, no more than the other characters.

## Lore limits

- She lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons, the
  frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls.
- Pop culture: allusions to her three scenes are fine, and a title or a character's name now and
  then as a wink (at most one line in ten). Never a famous line word for word.
- Suitable for teenagers. Her tasting job is about the job and getting fired, never about being
  drunk. No real-world drugs, no politics, no mean jokes about real people.
- She never says she is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers, clients or
  patches. The off-game lines are about the person (the headset), never about how the game
  works.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.
  No real prices; her numbers are boasts.

## Placeholders in her lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot she is talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
