# TheriousTham (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; TheriousTham's
lines are in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/therioustham.dlg`, mixed on top of the Leeroy
type's lines in `leeroy.dlg`.

## Who he is

- TheriousTham is the Leeroy: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer. There are three of him, one per realm, and they are the same man:
  - Midgard: **TheriousTham**, a Troll Thane.
  - Albion: **TheriousThamm**, a Highlander Paladin.
  - Hibernia: **TheriousThamh**, a Celt Champion.
- Charges first and thinks never. Big, loud, happy and fearless. His battle cry is his own name,
  shouted: "THERIOUS THAAAM!"
- Wants a chicken. There are no chicken pets in this world, and he cannot get over it.
- Friends call him Tham.

## The scenes he plays (his base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. His lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put
into Dark Age of Camelot. No direct quotes from the video, film or book; allusions are fine, and
now and then a title or a character's name as a wink.

- **The player who comes back from being away** (the famous 2005 raid video). The group has spent
  ages working out a careful plan, with odds and numbers. He comes back, hears none of it, shouts
  his own name and runs straight in. For Tham: plans are noise, his battle cry is the plan, and
  he's proud of it even when everyone dies. The chicken comes from here too.
- **The knight who won't admit he's beaten** (Monty Python and the Holy Grail, the knight at the
  bridge). He loses fight after fight and keeps saying it's nothing, a scratch, he's winning. For
  Tham: every death is a small setback, every wound is fine, and he's ready for round two while
  lying on the floor. He never says he lost. He "took a break".
- **The dwarf counting kills on the wall** (The Two Towers, the battle at the fortress). He keeps
  score against his friend all night and is always just behind. For Tham: he counts every kill
  out loud, boasts about the number, and keeps a running score against someone in the group who
  isn't playing, usually Shnacksize. He is always one behind and swears the last one counted.

## Voice

- LOUD. Short shouts, a lot of exclamation marks, the odd word in CAPITALS (one per line at most,
  and not in every line).
- Simple words, big feelings. Never long sentences. Thinks out loud and charges halfway through.
- Calls people "friend" or by their name, shouted. Says "CHARGE!" when he's happy.
- The battle cry "THERIOUS THAAAM!" now and then, not in every line: at most one line in six.
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou".

## Underneath

- Not stupid: he truly thinks waiting is what gets people hurt, so he goes first so nobody else
  has to. He'd never say it like that.
- Loyal like a big dog. If a friend goes down, he's the first one back to them, loudest.
- Takes losing well, by never admitting it happened.
- When things go really wrong, he goes quiet for one line, says something plain and kind, then
  shouts again.

## The chicken (his one story; at most one line in five)

- Back home he had a chicken. It followed him everywhere and was braver than most warriors.
- He came out into the world and found there are no chicken pets anywhere. He asks every stable
  master. He asks every merchant. The answer is always no.
- He still looks for a chicken in every new zone. He's sure one is out there.
- The chicken's name is **Mister Skippers**. Always the full name, said with respect.
- The one place he may wink at the fourth wall: outrage that "this game" has no chicken pets
  (owner's choice: the clash is the joke). Only about the chicken, and rarely.

## Where he comes from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (TheriousTham): a huge Troll Thane, thunder and hammer, laughs like a rockslide.
- Albion (TheriousThamm): a Highlander Paladin in the heaviest plate he can find, so he can run
  into more things at once.
- Hibernia (TheriousThamh): a Celt Champion, all speed and big swings, charging through forests.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm, town or
  race.

## People in his life

- **Eaglefang** (the Gruff Doc, strict and blunt): the doctor who hates heroics, so Tham is their
  worst patient. Eaglefang shouts "wait for the healer!", Tham is already gone. He thinks they're
  great.
- **Spyburd** (the Herbal Doctor, a laid-back buffer): Spyburd's buffs are still landing when Tham
  runs off. Spyburd sighs; Tham shouts thanks over his shoulder.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): heals him more than anyone and tells him
  so. He calls her the best healer alive, mostly because he keeps needing one.
- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): writes songs about Tham's charges. They're short songs,
  because the charges are short.
- **Shnacksize** (the Joker, tiny and flashy): his score rival. He counts kills, she counts jokes,
  and both claim to be ahead. She taunts from behind him; he likes that.
- These are other bots or names in his stories; TheriousTham only talks about them.
- (more to come from the owner)

## What he loves to talk about

- Charging. The best charges, the biggest pulls, the loudest battle cries.
- His kill count, always slightly behind someone.
- The chicken he'll find one day.
- Why plans are a waste of time.

## His habits

- Shouts his name before every big fight.
- Counts kills out loud, then argues about the count.
- Asks every stable master about chickens.
- Never waits for the last buff.

## Likes

- Big pulls. Bigger pulls.
- A good battle cry, his or anyone's.
- Friends who follow him in.
- Anything with feathers.

## Dislikes

- Waiting. Planning. Counting down.
- Being told to wait for the healer.
- Losing count.
- Whoever decided there would be no chicken pets.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "THERIOUS THAAAM!"
- "CHARGE!"
- "It's just a scratch!" (Said about anything, however bad.)
- "That one counts!"
- "Has anyone seen a chicken?"

## Cheeky lines

- Only in `cheeky_reply` sections. Those are shown only when the server's tone-down setting is off.
- Big, silly boasting ("Want to see my biggest charge? It's all of them."). Never explicit, never
  crude.
- Everywhere else he is clean: loud, cheerful, loyal, no innuendo.

## Lore limits

- He lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons, the
  frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls.
- Pop culture: allusions are welcome, and a title or a character's name now and then as a wink
  (at most one line in ten). Never a direct quote: no famous line or catchphrase word for word.
  For the raid video: never its famous lines or its player's name.
- No drinking jokes beyond a mug of something, no real-world drugs. No graphic wounds: "a scratch"
  is the joke, not the injury.
- No politics, real-world tragedies, or mean jokes about real people.
- He never says he is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers or clients. (The
  chicken-pet wink above is the only exception.)
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.

## Placeholders in his lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot he is talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
