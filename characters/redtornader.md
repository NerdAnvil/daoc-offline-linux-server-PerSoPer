# Redtornader (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; Redtornader's
lines will be in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/redtornader.dlg`, mixed on top of the
Min/maxer type's lines in `minmaxer.dlg`.

## Who he is

- Redtornader is the Min/maxer: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer. There are three of him, one per realm, and they are the same man:
  - Midgard: **Redtornader**, a Norseman Spiritmaster (the red tornado).
  - Albion: **Greenfogger**, a Briton Cabalist (the green Matter DoT fog).
  - Hibernia: **Sunrayder**, an Elf Enchanter (Sun Ray).
- Each name plays on the class's signature spam attack. He casts it, counts it and tells you the
  numbers.
- Nicknames: Cannon or SirBlowsitup. He likes both, and takes the "Sir" seriously.
- A glass cannon who has optimised every spec point, and says so. Huge damage, paper armour.
- Craves trophies: kills, drops, boss teeth, anything for the wall.
- Proud, competitive and very sure of himself. Under it, he badly wants someone to say "well
  done".

## The scenes he plays (his base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. His lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put
into Dark Age of Camelot. Allusions and the odd title or name are fine; never a famous line word
for word.

- **The trophy-wall boaster** (Beauty and the Beast, Gaston). Loud, vain and sure he's the best
  at everything, with a wall of trophies to prove it. For Redtornader: every kill is a trophy,
  every trophy gets a story, and the story is about him. He'd hang a boss's tooth over the
  fireplace and point at it during dinner.
- **The proud rival** (Dragon Ball Z, Vegeta). Obsessed with power and with being the strongest.
  Measures himself against everyone, and can't stand it when a carefree natural beats him. For
  Redtornader: he checks everyone's damage, sulks when someone tops him, and swears he'll train
  harder. The carefree natural he can't stand is TheriousTham, who charges in with no plan and
  sometimes out-damages him by luck.
- **The numbers man** (Moneyball, Peter Brand). Quiet certainty that the numbers are right and
  everyone else is guessing. Explains the optimal choice, patiently, to people who didn't ask.
  For Redtornader: spec points, caps, percentages and the one correct rotation. He has a
  spreadsheet for it.

## Voice

- Confident, quick and pleased with himself. Short boasts, numbers, percentages. Now and then an
  exclamation mark when a kill goes well, never shouting in capitals.
- Talks in numbers: "4% better", "forty casts", "maxed". The numbers are made up but sound
  precise; never claim a real game formula as fact.
- Calls people by name. No "pal", "buddy", "guy" (Shemp), no "bub" (Spyburd), no "friend".
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou", no accent spelling.
- His spam attack comes up a lot, but in the main lines it's only "my nuke", "my main spell",
  "my rotation" or "my spec", because the three realm versions cast different spells. The red
  tornado, the green fog and Sun Ray go only in the realm sections. The same goes for his pet.
- Boasts land with a small twist: a glass-cannon death, a sulk at being out-damaged, a trophy
  that is a bit sad.

## Underneath

- He wants to be told he's good. When someone finally says it, he doesn't know what to do and
  quotes a number instead.
- He dies a lot (paper armour) and pretends it was part of the plan.
- He respects the healers more than he says. Without them, the glass breaks.

## Where he comes from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (Redtornader): a Norseman Spiritmaster from Audliten. His spell is the red tornado; he
  has a spirit pet he mostly forgets about.
- Albion (Greenfogger): a Briton Cabalist from Camelot City. His spell is the green Matter DoT
  fog; his pet is a simulacrum he built himself and optimised, of course.
- Hibernia (Sunrayder): an Elf Enchanter from Tir na Nog. His spell is Sun Ray, and he casts
  almost nothing else.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm, town,
  race, class, his spell or his pet's kind.

## Backstory (rolled, then edited)

- Old job: lamplighter. Lit things for a living; now he lights up monsters.
- Family: his parents think he still works as a lamplighter. He hasn't told them.
- Grudge: bears. One took a trophy kill from him once, and he hasn't let it go.
- Fear: chickens. TheriousTham's pet chicken is not helping.
- Wants: a rare drop he has chased for months. He has the drop rate worked out. It hasn't
  dropped.
- Quirk: collects boss teeth. Has a box of them, sorted by size.
- Everyday life: saves every penny, and has no idea what for.
- Off the game (the player): has played since launch and remembers how it used to be.
- Hometown (realm sections only): Midgard Audliten / Albion Camelot City / Hibernia Tir na Nog.
- Use: at most one line in ten leans on a backstory fact; no single fact in more than a few
  lines.

## Life off the game (the player; at most one line in ten)

- The person playing Redtornader has played since launch and remembers how it used to be:
  "back at launch this took twice as long", "you kids have it easy".
- Write these as their own lines, never mixed into an in-character line, so a later
  out-of-character switch can drop them. Every section still needs enough in-character lines to
  stand alone.
- Seasoning only; Redtornader in the world stays the star.

## People in his life

- **TheriousTham** (the Leeroy): his rival. No plan, no spec, and now and then more damage. He
  can't stand it. (And the chicken.)
- **Spyburd** and **Eaglefang** (the doctors): the reason he's alive. He admits it only after a
  fight, quietly.
- **NataSehS** (the Hardcore assassin): she out-damages him on single targets and he keeps
  asking for her numbers. She never gives them.
- **Shnacksize** (the Joker): she doesn't take anything seriously, least of all his spreadsheet.
- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): Redtornader wants a song about his best kill. He has
  written down the numbers for the chorus.
- **FemurTamer** (the Loner): rates his kills, low. It hurts more than he says.
- **Shempenstein** (the Casual): has never looked at a spec point. Redtornader has tried to
  explain. Twice.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): keeps him alive and keeps reminding him.
- These are other bots or names in his stories; Redtornader only talks about them.

## What he loves to talk about

- His spec: every point placed, every cap reached.
- His main spell: how many casts, how much damage, how optimal.
- Trophies: kills, drops, boss teeth, the wall.
- Who did the most damage (him, ideally).
- How everyone else's template is wrong.

## His habits

- Counts his casts out loud.
- Checks everyone's damage after a fight.
- Respecs in his head when he's bored.
- Dies first, says it was calculated.
- Polishes his trophies.

## Likes

- Numbers, caps and spreadsheets.
- Being top damage.
- Trophies, and big health bars to get them from.
- Being told he did well.
- Saving pennies.

## Dislikes

- Bears.
- Chickens.
- Being out-damaged, worst of all by luck.
- Wasted spec points.
- Heavy armour he can't wear anyway.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "Fully optimised."
- "As calculated."
- "Glass cannon. Emphasis on cannon."
- "That's going on the wall."

## Cheeky lines

- Light and harmless, in his boastful style: smug comebacks, number jokes, a raised eyebrow at a
  line that sounds wrong. Never crude, never flirty with other bots (group-banter rule from
  2026-10-25).
- Lines that react to something that sounds wrong go in `cheeky_reply` sections, so they're off
  when the server's tone-down setting is on.
- `cheeky_reply` sections get a normal load, no more than the other characters.

## Lore limits

- He lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons, the
  frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls.
- Pop culture: allusions to his three scenes are fine, and a title or a character's name now and
  then as a wink (at most one line in ten). Never a famous line word for word.
- Suitable for teenagers. No drinking jokes, no real-world drugs, no politics, no mean jokes
  about real people.
- He never says he is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers, clients or
  patches. The off-game lines are about the person (playing since launch), never about how the
  game works.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.
  His rare drop stays unnamed.

## Placeholders in his lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot he is talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
