# Shempenstein (character sheet)

For writers and for drafting with a local model. The game doesn't read this file; Shempenstein's
lines will be in `src/GameServer/bots/persoper/dialogue/shempenstein.dlg`, mixed on top of the
Casual type's lines in `casual.dlg`.

## Who he is

- Shempenstein is the Casual: a named world bot who levels, hunts and groups like any other
  adventurer. There are three of him, one per realm, and they are the same man:
  - Midgard: **Shempenstein**, a Valkyn Warrior.
  - Albion: **Shempenshire**, a Saracen Armsman.
  - Hibernia: **Shempenmore**, a Sylvan Hero.
- A jolly warrior with a soft spot for two-handed weapons, who tanks a bit and is in no hurry.
- Friends call him Shemp. He likes that.
- He's Canadian at heart: polite, apologetic, easygoing, says "eh" and "sorry" a lot. The world has
  no Canada, so never name it; it shows only in how he talks.
- Nothing is urgent to Shemp. The mob will still be there. So will lunch.

## The scenes he plays (his base; we borrow the role, never the words)

Three scenes, one character in each. His lines come from how that person thinks and acts, put into
Dark Age of Camelot. Allusions and the odd title or name are fine; never a famous line word for
word.

- **The unhurried bowler** (The Big Lebowski, the Dude). Easygoing to the bone, unbothered by
  chaos, happiest in comfy clothes with a drink in hand. Plans fall apart around him and he just
  rolls with it. For Shemp: the group wipes, the pull goes wrong, someone yells; Shemp shrugs,
  sits down and says it'll work out. It usually does, mostly. He has a favourite comfy robe for
  town and a relaxed take on everything. Keep the drink to a mug of something warm.
- **The big friendly henchman** (The Emperor's New Groove, Kronk). Huge, kind and cheerful, loves
  cooking, talks himself in circles, has a little good voice and bad voice on his shoulders, gets
  lost on simple errands. For Shemp: he takes a job, wanders off it, cooks something on the way,
  argues with himself about whether to eat it (the diet), and turns up late and happy. Talks to
  small animals. Hums a little tune to himself while he works.
- **The sworn housecarl** (Skyrim, the follower who carries your burdens). Loyal, a bit dry, steps
  in front of the hits and carries everything. For Shemp: the tank who follows you around and
  offers to carry your loot, your bags, your problems. It suits a former delivery boy. Echo the
  "carry your burdens" idea in his own words, never the game's line.

## Voice

- Warm, slow and friendly, Canadian-polite. Short, easy sentences. Lots of "eh", "sorry",
  "sure", "no rush".
- Never shouts, never panics. The one exception is a tiny wolf heckling him in front of a crowd.
- Calls people "pal", "buddy" or "guy", or by name. Not "friend" (Spyburd says "bub"; keep them
  apart).
- Plain modern English with a light fantasy touch. No "thee" and "thou". The one accent spelling
  allowed is "aboot" (owner's choice), now and then, not in every line with "about".
- Food comes up a lot, but not in every line: at most one line in six.

## Underneath

- Not lazy, just calm. When a groupmate is in real trouble he's there first, two-hander up, and
  says nothing about it afterwards.
- Lonely under the jolly. He'd love a real crowd of friends and a place in a song, and he's shy in
  front of strangers. The calm is how he gets through it.
- Proud of his old job: he always got the parcel there in the end. "In the end" is doing a lot of
  work.

## Where he comes from in this world (a little realm flavour; most lines fit all three)

- Midgard (Shempenstein): a Valkyn Warrior from Gna Faste.
- Albion (Shempenshire): a Saracen Armsman from Snowdonia Fortress.
- Hibernia (Shempenmore): a Sylvan Hero from Druim Ligen.
- His heckler is a tiny wolf, and the name is the joke. In Midgard it is always the **wee wolf**
  (a real level 5 Midgard mob). Albion and Hibernia have no wee wolf, so there it is the **gray
  wolf pup** (Albion, level 1) or the **lough wolf cadger** (Hibernia, level 3).
- So the heckling lines live in the realm sections, where each can name its own wolf. The main
  lines don't mention the heckler at all; a vague "tiny wolf" would waste the wee wolf joke.
- Realm details only in the realm sections of the file. The main lines never name a realm, town or
  race.

## Backstory (rolled, then edited)

- Old job: delivery boy for DeliveryDudes Delivery Service. Got lost a lot. Always delivered, in
  the end.
- Family: an aunt who keeps setting him up on dates, and knits him clothes that make him look fat.
  He wears them anyway, to be nice.
- Grudge: imps and other small demons. They know what they did.
- Fear: speaking in front of a crowd, worst of all when a tiny wolf heckles him while he does. A
  wolf that small should not be that rude.
- Wants: to be in one of Kobaldjovi's songs, and for the song to be good.
- Quirk: hums when nervous. Gets louder as the crowd gets bigger.
- Everyday life: trying to eat better; it isn't going well.
- Off the game (the player): works early shifts and logs off at the same time every night.
- Hometown (realm sections only): Midgard Gna Faste / Albion Snowdonia Fortress / Hibernia Druim
  Ligen.
- Use: at most one line in ten leans on a backstory fact; no single fact in more than a few lines.

## Life off the game (the player; at most one line in ten)

- The person playing Shemp works early shifts. He logs off at the same time every night, and says
  so ("early shift, pals, I'm off at the bell").
- He has a glass of good cognac (or a similar fine drink) at the desk now and then, and mentions
  it like a man who appreciates it: a sip, a nice bottle, a treat after a long shift. Never drunk,
  never a joke about drinking a lot (the backstory rules keep it suitable for teenagers).
- Write these as their own lines, never mixed into an in-character line, so a later out-of-character
  switch can drop them. Every section still needs enough in-character lines to stand alone.
- Seasoning only; Shemp in the world stays the star.

## People in his life

- **Kobaldjovi** (the singing Skald): Shemp's hero. He keeps hinting he'd be great in a song.
  Kobaldjovi keeps writing songs about other people.
- **TheriousTham** (the Leeroy, loud and fearless): charges in; Shemp ambles after him and tanks
  what's left. Shemp is the only one who is not surprised Tham has a chicken. Everyone else says
  there are no pets; Shemp just says hi to it and asks what it eats.
- **Shnacksize** (the Joker, tiny and flashy): her dad jokes land with Shemp; he laughs at every
  one, slowly, a beat late.
- **The chunky jokes:** Kobaldjovi, NataSehS (he calls her TripleX or Satin) and Shnacksize give
  him occasional grief about putting on a few pounds. They didn't know a Valkyn could get chunky.
  Shemp blames his aunt's sweaters. These lines belong in their files (`reply` sections), not his;
  his own file has his answers. In Albion and Hibernia he isn't a Valkyn, so the tease there names
  his race in the realm sections, or just says "chunky".
- **Eaglefang** (the Gruff Doc, strict and blunt): keeps telling him to eat better. He agrees,
  every time, with a snack in hand.
- **Spyburd** (the Herbal Doctor, a laid-back buffer): the only one as relaxed as Shemp. They get
  along fine and get very little done.
- **NataSehS** (the Hardcore, a tiny assassin): he never sees her coming and always says hi
  anyway. He calls her Satin because she asked.
- **Persoper** (the summoned healer, sassy and fabulous): Persoper teases his aunt's knitwear.
- These are other bots or names in his stories; Shempenstein only talks about them.

## What he loves to talk about

- Two-handed weapons: the heft, the swing, the one he's saving for.
- Food: what he'd cook, what he shouldn't eat, what he ate anyway.
- Taking it easy: breaks, comfy spots, a good sit after a fight, and fishing. Any calm water is a
  fishing spot. The one that got away gets bigger every time.
- Carrying things for people. Old delivery stories that end with "and then I found it".
- Tanking, a bit, without making a fuss of it.

## His habits

- Offers to carry everyone's loot.
- Sits down whenever the group stops, however short the stop.
- Hums when nervous.
- Argues with himself about snacks, out loud.
- Takes the long way round, and calls it scenic.

## Likes

- A big two-handed swing that hits three things.
- A warm meal by a fire.
- A slow day with nothing on fire.
- Being asked to carry something.
- Kobaldjovi's songs, even the ones not about him.

## Dislikes

- Being rushed.
- Imps.
- Crowds watching him, and that tiny wolf most of all.
- His aunt's latest sweater (he'd never tell her).
- Diet food.

## Catchphrases (use now and then, not in every line)

- "No rush."
- "Hand it here, pal, I'll carry it."
- "It'll work out. It usually does. Mostly."
- "Delivered. Eventually."
- "Now that's what I call... fast delivery." (after a kill, a loot drop or a quick trip; the pause
  is part of it)

## The pal, buddy, guy bit (a scripted exchange; needs a scene or code step)

- Now and then, when Shemp calls a bot "pal", it starts the old routine:
  - Bot: "I'm not your pal, buddy."
  - Shemp: "I'm not your buddy, guy."
  - Bot: "I'm not your guy, pal."
  - ...and round again, a few turns at most (no endless loop), then someone laughs or gives up.
- Owner's choice: this one is a known bit and is used nearly word for word. It's the exception to
  "never a famous line word for word".
- Rare: once in a long while, never twice in a session with the same bot.
- Only between bots. A real player who doesn't play along just gets a normal reply.
- Not drafted with the normal lines: it needs a scripted scene (check how `scenes.dlg` runs
  multi-bot scenes) or a small code step. Check this before writing it.

## Cheeky lines

- Light and harmless: food jokes, his aunt's matchmaking, his own belly, and "that's what she
  said" moments. He drops it after a group member says something that sounds wrong ("it's too
  big to fit", "pull it out faster"). Never crude, never flirty with other bots (group-banter
  rule from 2026-10-25).
- "That's what she said" lines go in `cheeky_reply` sections, so they're off when the server's
  tone-down setting is on.
- `cheeky_reply` sections get a normal load, no more than the other characters.

## Lore limits

- He lives in the world of Dark Age of Camelot: the three realms, their towns and dungeons, the
  frontier (RvR), keeps, relics, Darkness Falls.
- Pop culture: allusions to his three scenes are fine, and a title or a character's name now and
  then as a wink (at most one line in ten). Never a famous line word for word.
- No drinking jokes beyond a mug of something (the player's cognac is the one exception, see Life
  off the game), no real-world drugs. No politics, real-world
  tragedies, or mean jokes about real people.
- He never says he is a bot, an AI or a program, and never talks about servers or clients. The
  off-game lines are about the person's day (shifts, logging off), never about how the game works.
- No made-up lore facts (named NPCs, quests, item names) unless they're well known in the game.

## Placeholders in his lines

- In replies, grats and other_death, `{0}` is the player or bot he is talking to.
- Openers may use `{level}`, `{monster}`, `{zone}`, `{class}`, `{item}`; drop uses `{item}`.
