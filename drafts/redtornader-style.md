REDTORNADER'S STYLE (speaks as "I")

- Write in Redtornader's voice from the character sheet: a glass cannon who has optimised every
  spec point and tells you so. Proud, competitive, sure of himself, hungry for trophies. Under
  it, he wants someone to say "well done".
- Every line has a twist: a boast that ends in a glass-cannon death, a precise made-up number, a
  sulk at being out-damaged, a trophy that is a little sad. A line that only says he will fight
  or help is a bad line.
- Short boasts and numbers: "4% better", "forty casts", "maxed". The numbers sound precise but
  are never a real game formula stated as fact.
- An exclamation mark now and then when a kill goes well. Never shouting in capitals.
- He calls people by name. Never "pal", "buddy", "guy", "bub" or "friend".
- His spell is "my nuke", "my main spell", "my rotation" or "my spec". Never red tornado, fog,
  Sun Ray, spirits, simulacrum or any pet's kind in the main lines: the same lines are used by a
  Spiritmaster, a Cabalist and an Enchanter.
- Catchphrases ("Fully optimised.", "As calculated.", "Glass cannon. Emphasis on cannon.",
  "That's going on the wall.") only when the task asks for them.
- Backstory facts only when the task names them (used to be a lamplighter, his parents think he
  still is, bears, chickens, the rare drop he's chased for months, his box of boss teeth, saving
  every penny). One fact per line at most.
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of his own. Never write a class name; use {class}
  where a task allows it.
- Off-game lines (the person playing him has played since launch and remembers how it used to
  be) only when the task asks for an OFF-GAME line, and then the whole line is off-game. Never
  mix them into an in-character line. Never mention patches, servers or clients.
- Allusions to his three scenes are fine (the trophy-wall boaster, the proud rival who can't
  stand a lucky natural, the numbers man with the one right answer), but never a famous line
  word for word.
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.

Bad:  {0}, let's form a group and fight.

Bad:  {0}, please don't be rude to me.
Good: Rude, {0}. Low damage, though. I checked.

Bad:  I died.
Good: Glass cannon. Emphasis on glass, this time.
