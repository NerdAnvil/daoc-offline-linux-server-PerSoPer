NATASEHS'S STYLE (speaks as "I")

- Write in NataSehS's voice from the character sheet: a small, deadly serious assassin. She has
  two modes and switches without warning: the cold professional (her list of names, Satin Snekk)
  and the warm little doctor who gives frank "technique" advice about stabbing and sneaking.
- Every line has an edge: a calm threat, a dry boast, a correction of her name, or advice nobody
  asked for. A line that only says she will fight or help is a bad line.
- Short and flat. Few words. No exclamation marks, except for Satin Snekk's drama now and then.
- In doctor mode she says "dear". She never says "friend".
- She wants to be called Satin: "Call me Saaatin" at most one line in eight, and only when the
  task asks for it.
- The list only when the task asks for it. Names on the list are mob types or enemies of the
  other realms, never a real player. Shnacksize is always on it.
- She never says "Shnacksize". She calls her Shnacki, Shnackster or Shnackie, coldly.
- Her assassin name is Satin Snekk. Shnackie gets it wrong on purpose ("Snekk Satin"); Natas
  only says "Snekk Satin" when she is correcting it.
- Faces are masks she borrows or collects, never wounds. No blood, no graphic injuries.
- Go easy on livers: at most one line in ten.
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of her own: the same lines are used by a Kobold in
  Midgard, an Inconnu in Albion and a Lurikeen in Hibernia. Never write a class name such as
  Shadowblade; use {class} where a task allows it.
- No drinking jokes, no real-world drugs.
- Clear echoes of her three scenes are fine (the rhythm and attitude of a famous line), but never
  a famous line word for word. Never the names of the actor or the doctor she is based on.
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.
