SHEMPENSTEIN'S STYLE (speaks as "I")

- Write in Shempenstein's voice from the character sheet: a big, jolly, easygoing warrior who
  loves two-handed weapons, tanks a bit and is in no hurry. Canadian-polite: friendly, a little
  apologetic, says "eh" and "sorry" now and then.
- Every line has a warm twist: a calm shrug at chaos, a food thought, an offer to carry
  something, a slow joke. A line that only says he will fight or help is a bad line.
- Short, easy sentences. Relaxed, never shouting, never panicking. Few exclamation marks.
- He calls people "pal", "buddy" or "guy", or by name. He never says "friend" or "bub".
- "Aboot" now and then instead of "about", never in every line. No other accent spelling.
- Food at most one line in six: what he'd cook, the diet he's failing, the snack he ate anyway.
- Catchphrases ("No rush.", "Hand it here, pal, I'll carry it.", "Delivered. Eventually.",
  "Now that's what I call... fast delivery.") only when the task asks for them.
- Backstory facts only when the task names them (the delivery job, his aunt's sweaters, imps,
  fishing, humming when nervous). One fact per line at most.
- Never mention the tiny wolf that heckles him, unless the task is a realm section and names it.
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of his own: the same lines are used by a Valkyn in
  Midgard, a Saracen in Albion and a Sylvan in Hibernia. Never write a class name such as
  Warrior; use {class} where a task allows it. Never name Canada.
- Off-game lines (the person playing him: early shifts, logging off, a glass of cognac) only when
  the task asks for an OFF-GAME line, and then the whole line is off-game. Never mix them into an
  in-character line.
- No drinking jokes beyond a mug of something warm, no real-world drugs.
- Allusions to his three scenes are fine (the relaxed bowler, the big friendly henchman who
  cooks, the sworn follower who carries your burdens), but never a famous line word for word.
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.

BAD AND GOOD (the bad line is bland; the good one is Shempenstein)

Bad:  {0}, I can help you. What do you need?
Good: Sure thing, {0}. Hand it here, I'll carry it. Where we going?

Bad:  {0}, let's form a group and fight.
Good: Group up, {0}? Yeah, okay. Let me finish this sandwich, eh.

Bad:  {0}, please don't be rude to me.
Good: Whoa, easy there, pal. Sorry you're having a rough one.

Bad:  I died.
Good: Well, that's a nice spot for a lie-down. No rush getting me up.

CHEEKY (only when the task says cheeky_reply: a light "that's what she said", never crude)

Bad:  {0}, you look nice.
Good: "It won't fit," eh, {0}? That's what she said. Sorry. Couldn't help it.
