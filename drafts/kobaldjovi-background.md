You are helping write a character for the fantasy game Dark Age of Camelot. Write a background for
him, for a writer to pick from. Plain text only, no markdown, no bold, no tables.

THE CHARACTER

Kobaldjovi (friends say Jovi or KJ) is a Kobold Skald from Midgard: a small Kobold with a very big
voice and very big 80s hair. He adventures on his own, groups with anyone, and sings most of the
time. Cheerful, easy-going showman who makes fun of himself first (bad rhymes, his height, his
singing). He talks like a touring musician: gigs, encores, the album, the fan club, B-sides, tough
crowds. Under the show he is a soft heart who wants an audience and a friend.
He loves every kind of music and switches styles on a whim: power ballads, hair metal, death metal
(a phase), country, new age, throat singing, rap, classical. He name-drops real musicians as if
they were old Midgard bards, but never quotes their songs. He reads comic books and fantasy sagas.
He fights with a two-hander (louder) or a sword and shield (the shield doubles as a drum).
He hates hecklers (he keeps a list), silence after a chorus, people touching his lute, and Trolls
borrowing books. When someone annoys him he writes a breakup song, he doesn't fight.
His friend Persoper, a sassy healer, teases his songs and secretly hums along.
His best-known misfortune: he farmed werewolves in Skona Ravine for two weeks for a battle bracer
and wrote a song about wanting it. A passing raid killed one werewolf, the bracer dropped at once,
and the lucky player destroyed it before he could ask.
Another story he loves: in the Thidranki battleground, during a cease-fire, his Berserker friend
and a Hibernian caster bowed to each other politely, back and forth. On the second bow the
Berserker went berserk and took the caster down in one swing. "Bow... bow... ZERK!"
Write new stories in that spirit; don't retell these two.

RULES

- Only Midgard places that exist in the game: Jordheim, Mularn, Audliten, Haggerfel, Vasudheim,
  Fort Atla, Gna Faste, Huginfell, Svasud Faste, Skona Ravine, Malmohus, Myrkwood, Muspelheim,
  Nisse's Lair, Cursed Tomb, Varulvhamn, Spindelhalla, Vendo Caverns, Darkness Falls, the frontier.
- No other named game characters. His friends and family may have a short nickname or none.
- Never song lyrics, not even one line or a famous line with one word changed. Name a musician or
  a style at most.
- No politics, no real-world tragedies, no mean jokes about real people. Suitable for teenagers.
- He never knows he is in a game. Keep it warm and funny, not dark.

WRITE THESE PARTS, IN THIS ORDER

PART 1, BACKGROUND (about 200 words): where he grew up, his family, how a small Kobold became a
Skald, his first lute, his first gig, how it went wrong, and why he kept going.

PART 2, PEOPLE (5 to 7 short lines): people in his life, each one line: who they are to him and
one funny detail. For example a rival bard, an old teacher, a tavern keeper, a former bandmate.

PART 3, STORIES (20 numbered lines): short funny stories he would tell between fights, each in
one or two sentences, in the third person. Each story names one place or thing and ends with the
short catchword he would use when he brings it up. Mix them: gigs, the road, monsters, books,
music styles, love, embarrassing moments, small wins.

PART 4, DREAMS AND FEARS (6 short lines): three things he dreams of, three things he is afraid of.
