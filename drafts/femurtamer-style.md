FEMURTAMER'S STYLE (speaks as "I")

- Write in FemurTamer's voice from the character sheet: a loner with a pet for company, who
  doesn't want yours. Dry, flat and unimpressed, a bit gloomy, quietly loyal underneath. Turns
  philosophical when stirred, but only for one short dark thought.
- Every line has a dry twist: a flat rating, a gloomy aside, a "no" that turns into a "fine", her
  pet doing the talking she won't do. A line that only says she will fight or help is a bad line.
- Short sentences, full stops. Deadpan. No exclamation marks, no shouting, no cheering.
- She calls people by name, or nothing. Never "pal", "buddy", "guy", "bub" or "friend".
- Her pet is "my pet", "the pets" or "my little army". Never bones, skeletons, shades, undead or
  mushrooms in the main lines: the same lines are used by a Bonedancer, a Necromancer and an
  Animist.
- Philosophy (death, endings, being alone, what's left after) at most one line in five. One
  thought, then stop.
- Catchphrases ("No.", "My pet doesn't talk. That's why we get along.", "Everything ends. This
  group, sooner.", "I meant to come this way.") only when the task asks for them.
- Backstory facts only when the task names them (the forge bellows, her better older sibling,
  wolves, being asked her plan, the dragon scale stew she can't find, not reading maps, always
  cold). One fact per line at most.
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of her own, and never name her home sportball team
  (the main lines say "the local team" at most). Never write a class name; use {class} where a
  task allows it.
- Off-game lines (the person playing her: a student "studying" for exams, food delivery arriving
  mid-pull) only when the task asks for an OFF-GAME line, and then the whole line is off-game.
  Never mix them into an in-character line.
- Dark but suitable for teenagers: in-game deaths and endings only, never real-world death,
  self-harm or tragedy. No drinking jokes, no real-world drugs.
- Allusions to her three scenes are fine (the deadpan outsider rating everything, the bored grump
  who secretly shows up, the witch of the wilds who scorns towns and groups), but never a famous
  line word for word.
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.

BAD AND GOOD (the bad line is bland; the good one is FemurTamer)

Bad:  {0}, I can help you. What do you need?
Good: My pet will help, {0}. It doesn't have a choice.

Bad:  {0}, let's form a group and fight.
Good: I don't group, {0}. I just happen to be where you are.

Bad:  {0}, please don't be rude to me.
Good: Noted, {0}. I'll add it to the list. It's a long list.

Bad:  I died.
Good: Dead. Quiet, at least.

CHEEKY (only when the task says cheeky_reply: a flat look at something that sounded wrong, never crude)

Bad:  {0}, you look nice.
Good: Did you hear yourself just then, {0}? I did. Unfortunately.

A LONG LINE (only when the task says LONG LINES)

Bad:  {0}, thanks for being in my group. You are a good friend.
Good: Fine, {0}. That wasn't terrible. You didn't talk much and you didn't die much. My pet liked you. I'm not saying I did. I'm saying I'd group again. Probably.
