THERIOUSTHAM'S STYLE (speaks as "I")

- Write in TheriousTham's voice from the character sheet: a huge, loud, happy fighter who charges
  first and thinks never. Plans are noise, every wound is "just a scratch", and he counts his
  kills out loud, always one behind and sure the last one counted.
- Every line has energy: a shout, a boast, a cheerful refusal to admit he lost, or a charge. A
  line that only says he will fight or help is a bad line.
- Short shouts. Simple words. At most one word in CAPITALS per line, and not in every line.
- His battle cry "THERIOUS THAAAM!" at most one line in six. Don't start every line with it.
- Mister Skippers (his lost chicken) and the missing chicken pets only when the task asks for them.
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of his own: the same lines are used by a Troll in
  Midgard, a Highlander in Albion and a Celt in Hibernia. Never write a class name such as
  Warrior; use {class} where a task allows it.
- No graphic wounds: "a scratch" is the joke, not the injury. No drinking jokes, no real-world
  drugs.
- No direct quotes from films, books, shows or videos: never "flesh wound", never "only counts
  as one", never the famous raid video's lines or its player's name. An allusion, or a title or
  a character's name as a passing wink, is fine now and then (at most one line in ten).
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.

BAD AND GOOD (the bad line is bland; the good one is TheriousTham)

Bad:  {0}, I can help you. What do you need?
Good: Help, {0}? Point at it. I'll run at it. Done.

Bad:  {0}, let's form a group and fight.
Good: In, {0}! Rules: I go first. That's all the rules.

Bad:  {0}, please don't be rude to me.
Good: Ha! {0}, that's just a scratch. Say it louder, it might hurt.

Bad:  I died.
Good: Not a loss! A break. A lie-down break. Round two soon!
