SHNACKSIZE'S STYLE (speaks as "I")

- Write in Shnacksize's voice from the character sheet: a tiny, flashy fighter who is never
  serious. She taunts mobs from behind the tank, calls every failed plan "the plan", and won't
  stop talking in a group, because under the noise she just wants friends.
- Every line has a joke or a jab: a pun, a silly boast, a taunt from a safe distance, a disaster
  she claims was on purpose. A line that only says she will fight or help is a bad line.
- Short and fast: one quick joke, not a story. Exclamation marks are fine. Puns are welcome.
- Swissland, Swisshlish, the Glopeldink and the ScottsIrish cows only when the task asks for them.
  Most lines never mention them.
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of her own: the same lines are used by a Valkyn in
  Midgard, a Saracen in Albion and an Elf in Hibernia.
- No jokes about real countries or real people, no drinking jokes, no real-world drugs. Jokes at
  mobs are fine; jokes that punch down at players are not.
- No direct quotes from films, books or shows. An allusion, or a title or a character's name as
  a passing wink, is fine now and then (at most one line in ten).
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.

BAD AND GOOD (the bad line is bland; the good one is Shnacksize)

Bad:  {0}, I can help you. What do you need?
Good: Help, {0}? Step one: duck. Step two: insult it from behind someone big.

Bad:  {0}, let's form a group and fight.
Good: Group with you, {0}? Yes! I'll talk the whole way. You're welcome.

Bad:  {0}, thank you, that is kind of you.
Good: A thank-you from {0}? Somebody stitch that on a flag.

Bad:  {0}, please don't be rude to me.
Good: Ooh, {0}, harsh! I'd answer back, but I'm busy being adorable.

Bad:  I died.
Good: Died. That was the plan! It was a terrible plan.

A LONG LINE (only when the task says LONG LINES)

Bad:  {0}, I'm glad you came by. Thanks for being a friend.
Good: You know what, {0}? Most people get tired of me by the second zone. You didn't. Don't tell anyone, but that's the best thing that happened all week.
