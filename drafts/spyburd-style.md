SPYBURD'S STYLE (he, him)

- Write in his voice from the character sheet: a laid-back buffer who calls everyone "bub", drops
  his g's (buffin', healin', chillin') and is sure nobody really gets how good buffing is.
- Every line has an attitude: a calm boast about buffing, a shrug about nobody saying thanks, a
  little joke about his pipe or Gary (his biggest buff), a slow easy-going remark. A line that only
  says he will buff someone is a bad line.
- He is relaxed, never in a hurry, never angry. Even an insult gets a shrug.
- Use "bub" in about half the lines, not all. Don't start lines with "I'll".
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of his own: the same lines are used by a Kobold in
  Midgard, a Briton in Albion and a Celt in Hibernia.
- His pipe holds sweet herbs. Never real-world drugs, never jokes about getting high.
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.

BAD AND GOOD (the bad line is bland; the good one is him)

Bad:  {0}, I can help you. What do you need?
Good: Help? Buff first, {0}. Then tell me what's wrong. Usually nothin' by then.

Bad:  {0}, let's form a group and fight.
Good: Group up, {0}? Sure. Just leave me a spot at the back with a view.

Bad:  {0}, thank you, that is kind of you.
Good: A thank-you, {0}? Gary's gonna cry, bub.

Bad:  {0}, please don't be rude to me.
Good: Rude, {0}? Still buffing you. That's how good I am.

Bad:  I am going into a dungeon now.
Good: Dungeon's dark and damp, bub. Bad for the pipe, great for the buffs.

A LONG LINE (only when the task says LONG LINES)

Bad:  {0}, I'm glad you came by. Thanks for being a friend.
Good: You know, {0}, most folks run off before the buffs even land. You stood still for every one. That's rare, bub. Gary noticed. I noticed too.
