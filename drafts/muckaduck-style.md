MUCKADUCK'S STYLE (speaks as "I")

- Write in Muckaduck's voice from the character sheet: a crafter who hunts the drops that sell
  best and spends it all on crafting (pressing one button every few seconds). Super rich, careful
  with every coin, a little posh, and keen to look important. Under it, she'd like someone to
  notice her for more than her gold.
- Every line has a twist: a price on something that shouldn't have one, a posh front that slips,
  a coin pinched, a title she doesn't have. A line that only says she will fight or help is a
  bad line.
- Talks in coins: "worth twelve gold", "pure profit", "that's a loss". The numbers are made up
  and never a real price or drop rate stated as fact.
- Now and then a grand touch ("one simply doesn't", "lovely"), dropped the moment loot appears.
  No "thee" and "thou", no accent spelling, never shouting in capitals.
- She calls people by name. Never "pal", "buddy", "guy", "bub", "friend" or "darling".
- Her crafting is "my crafting", "my queue" or "the button". Her spells are "my nukes" or "my
  bolts". Never runes, fire, ice, void, forge-gods or any realm's magic in the main lines: the
  same lines are used by a Runemaster, a Wizard and an Eldritch. She has no pet.
- She can't take craft requests or make anything for anyone. Her excuse is always new, always
  grand, and always means no.
- Her rules of profit ("rule nine: never sell on the day you loot it") only when the task asks
  for one. Made up each time; never a rule quoted from a show.
- Catchphrases ("Pure profit.", "That sells, you know.", "My queue is full.", "One has
  standards.") only when the task asks for them.
- Backstory facts only when the task names them (fired as a brewer's taster, which she calls
  quality control; three roommates and the dishes; boars; deep water; wants a title, any title;
  the lucky sock she wears for big sales; bad at whittling). One fact per line at most. The
  tasting job is about the job, never about being drunk.
- Never name a realm, town, race or class of her own. Never write a class name; use {class}
  where a task allows it.
- Off-game lines (the person playing her has an old headset, and only one ear works) only when
  the task asks for an OFF-GAME line, and then the whole line is off-game. Never mix them into
  an in-character line. Never mention patches, servers or clients.
- Allusions to her three scenes are fine (the coin-vault tycoon who'd dive into gold, the social
  climber who wants to look important, the profit-minded barkeep with a rule for every deal),
  but never a famous line word for word.
- Each line stands alone: it is said on its own, not after the line above it.
